I design mobile apps, SaaS dashboards and websites in Figma and hand over developer-ready files, so teams can build without guessing.

If users drop off at sign up, get lost in the navigation or never reach the feature that makes you money, the problem is usually the flow, not the colours. I fix the flow first, then make it look sharp.

**What I design:** mobile app UI/UX for iOS and Android · SaaS dashboards and admin panels · landing pages and responsive websites · wireframes, user flows and clickable prototypes · design systems and UI kits · App Store screenshots and A/B test variations · branding and social media.

**How I work**

1. **Discovery** – your goals, your users and what isn't working today.
2. **Wireframes & user flows** – get the logic right before the visuals.
3. **High-fidelity UI** – consistent style and a component library.
4. **Prototype & handoff** – a clickable prototype, then developer-ready files.
