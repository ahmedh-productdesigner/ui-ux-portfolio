# Pedometer App Onboarding Redesign

![Pedometer App Onboarding Redesign cover](cover.svg)

**Category:** Mobile App Design · **Type:** iOS & Android · Health & Fitness · **Tool:** Figma

A redesign of the first-run onboarding for a step-counter app, focused on getting users to their first tracked walk.

## The challenge

Onboarding is where most fitness apps lose people. Permission requests and goal setup needed to feel quick and motivating, not like a form.

## What I designed

- Welcome and value screens
- Health permission priming
- Daily goal setup
- First-day home screen

## Approach

Explained why each permission matters before asking for it, and kept every screen to one decision.
