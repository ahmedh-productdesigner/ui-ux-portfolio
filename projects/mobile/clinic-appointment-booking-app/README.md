# Clinic Appointment Booking App

![Clinic Appointment Booking App cover](cover.svg)

**Category:** Mobile App Design · **Type:** iOS & Android · Healthcare · **Tool:** Figma

A patient-facing booking app that takes someone from "I need a doctor" to a confirmed appointment in a few taps.

## The challenge

Booking a clinic visit usually means phone calls, unclear availability and no reminders. The goal was a flow short enough that patients finish it on the first try.

## What I designed

- Doctor and specialty search with filters
- Real-time slot picker and booking confirmation
- Appointment history, reminders and rescheduling
- Patient profile and onboarding

## Approach

Started from the booking flow and cut every step that did not help the patient choose a time. Calm colours and large touch targets keep it easy to use for all ages.
