# Smart Home Mobile App

![Smart Home Mobile App cover](cover.svg)

**Category:** Mobile App Design · **Type:** iOS & Android · IoT · **Tool:** Figma

A control app for lights, climate, security and devices, organised by room so the right switch is always one tap away.

## The challenge

Smart home apps often bury everyday controls under device lists and settings. The task was to make the common actions instant.

## What I designed

- Home overview with room cards
- Device control screens (lights, AC, cameras, locks)
- Scenes and automation
- Energy usage view

## Approach

Grouped controls by room and by frequency of use, with clear on/off states and readable status at a glance.
