# Elite Bond Website

![Elite Bond Website – landing page and website UI/UX design by Muhammad Ahmed Hussain](cover.svg)

**Category:** Landing Pages & Websites · **Type:** Website · Corporate · **Tool:** Figma

A corporate website with a premium feel, built to present the business and generate enquiries.

## The challenge

Corporate sites tend to be text-heavy. The goal was a premium look with information that is easy to scan.

## What I designed

- Homepage
- Services
- About the company
- Contact

## Approach

Elegant typography, a restrained palette and generous spacing for a high-end feel.
