# Finance App Store Screenshots

![Finance App Store Screenshots cover](cover.svg)

**Category:** App Store Screenshots · **Type:** iOS & iPad · ASO · **Tool:** Figma

App Store screenshots for a financial management app, designed to communicate trust and key features fast.

## The challenge

Finance apps need to look secure and simple. The screenshots had to show value without crowding the screen.

## What I designed

- iPhone and iPad screenshot sets
- Feature headlines
- Brand-consistent styling
- Store-ready exports

## Approach

Clean layouts, one feature per frame and colour that matches the app's brand.
